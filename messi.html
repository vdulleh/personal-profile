Career Records

Some of Messi's biggest records

01

Most decorated

Messi has been recognized as the most decorated player in the history of professional football, with 46 team trophies as of July 10, 2026.

02

Barcelona scoring record

He is Barcelona's all-time leading scorer and holds the record for the most goals for a single club.

03

La Liga record scorer

Messi holds the all-time La Liga scoring record with 474 goals.

04

Calendar year record

He scored 91 goals in 2012, a famous world record for goals in a calendar year.

05

Argentina leader

He is Argentina's all-time leading scorer and one of the most capped players in international football history.

06

Playmaking record

Messi is also widely credited with the most official recorded assists in football history.

07

Unique longevity

He is the only player to win the Ballon d'Or across three different decades and the only one to win four straight Ballon d'Or awards.

08

Major tournament impact

Messi has earned best-player style honors at youth, continental, and World Cup level, underlining how complete his career has been across eras.
